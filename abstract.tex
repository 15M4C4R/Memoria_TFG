\chapter*{Abstract}
For many people born in the first decade of the 21st century, video games are established as one of the main leisure activities, deeply marking an entire generation. Digital entertainment evolves across different platforms, from accessible browser games powered by \textit{Adobe Flash} to major console productions and the closed ecosystems of mobile gaming. In this context, this Bachelor's Thesis, \textit{King and Peasant}, arises from an interest in video game architecture and the desire to invest academic effort into a creative and stimulating product. Furthermore, to overcome the typically isolated nature of university projects, it is proposed as a collaborative development between two people that simulates the dynamics and demands of a professional environment in the software industry. The central objective is the design, implementation, and deployment of the digital version of the asymmetric board game \textit{King and Peasant} on a multiplayer web platform, offering a persistent ecosystem through the use of modern technologies, ensuring a smooth, synchronized, and highly available experience.

To achieve this objective, the strategic card game \textit{King and Peasant} is adapted under the standards of an agile and iterative framework. This implementation is carried out employing modern technologies such as React, Express, Node.js, MariaDB, Redis, and WebSockets, under the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architectural pattern. As a result, a functional and dockerized web application is deployed, integrating a user management system, multiplayer waiting rooms (lobbies), and a real-time rules engine, demonstrating that it is possible to utilize current web technologies to deliver an interactive and smooth experience, supported by a solid and scalable technical foundation that meets the standards of professional software engineering.
